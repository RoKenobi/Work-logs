When doing large catchup passes for the work-logs vault (writing multiple weeks of dailies, rollups, entity registry updates), split into new sessions every ~3 weeks of content.

**Why:** The conversation context gets very long and the user finds it easier to track progress in fresh sessions.

**How to apply:** After completing a week block or biweekly, suggest starting a new session for the next block rather than continuing indefinitely in the same one.
